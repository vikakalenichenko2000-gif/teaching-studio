#Text: Saturday in the city
It's Saturday. Mia and her brother Leo are at the bus stop.
"Where are you going, Mia?" asks her friend Tom.
"We're going to the museum. We want to see old cars," says Mia.
"Are you going to the movie theater?" asks Tom.
"No, we aren't. We're going to the restaurant after the museum," says Leo.
"Cool! I'm going to the recreation center. I want to swim!" says Tom.
